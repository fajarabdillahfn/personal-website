Most things on the internet are trying to become something bigger. This site is starting in the opposite direction: small, useful, and easy to keep alive.

I wanted a place where a new idea could become a short note without needing a publishing workflow, a database, or a content calendar. A Markdown file is enough. A good link is enough. Sometimes a half-finished thought is enough too.

The site will change as I do. There will be projects, experiments, and writing here — some polished, some still finding their shape. That feels like a better record than waiting until everything looks finished.

For now, welcome. I’m glad you found this little corner.

## What comes next

- Add a few real projects.
- Write when there is something worth remembering.
- Keep the stack simple enough that publishing stays enjoyable.
